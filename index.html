About Me

Hi,
I’m A T Abhijith, a Software Developer who loves building things from the ground up. I got into web development simply because I was curious about how websites work—and that curiosity quickly turned into something I genuinely enjoy doing every day.

I mostly work with the MERN stack, where I like bringing backend logic and frontend design together in a clean, simple way. I believe good code should be easy to understand, easy to maintain, and actually useful in the real world.

I’m always learning and trying to improve my skills. Right now, I’m exploring Python and TypeScript to build faster and more reliable apps. I enjoy experimenting, solving problems, and working with people who are just as curious as I am.

I’m open to new opportunities and freelance work. If you have an idea you want to bring to life or just want to collaborate, feel free to reach out—I’d love to connect!
